Financial and security guardrails

QuFense blocks unsafe activation. QuCFA blocks unsustainable activation.

The contracted service must be fully covered without exceeding the customer tariff, and gross margin cannot fall below 30%. Missing provider, cost, evidence, recovery or acceptance data leaves the product blocked.
